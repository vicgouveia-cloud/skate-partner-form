# 🛹 Skate Buddy & Session Partner Intake Form

An interactive, responsive online intake form designed for skateboarders looking for session partners, progression homies, and filmers.

## Features

- **Skater Identity & Street Tag**: Contact info, social media handles, and optional skater ID photo upload.
- **Setup & Stance**: Board type, stance (Regular / Goofy / Mongo), deck width, wheel hardness, and experience level.
- **Terrains & Skill Matrix**: Comfort and skill ratings across Street, Skatepark Plazas, Bowls/Pools, Miniramp/Vert, Flatground, and Downhill.
- **Tricks & Progression**: Go-to signature tricks, battles in progress, dream combo lines, and video clips/links.
- **Spots & Session Times**: Favorite skateparks, session time badges, spot hunting transportation, and filming gear.
- **Vibe & Soundtrack**: Session soundtrack preferences (90s Boom-Bap, Skate Punk, Indie, Trap, etc.) and partner expectations.
- **Digital Skater Tag / Signature**: HTML5 Canvas signature pad for digital signatures or graffiti tags.
- **Free Backend & PDF Export**: Integrated with FormSubmit.co for direct email delivery with table formatting and print/PDF support.

## Live Deployment

Once pushed to GitHub, enable **GitHub Pages** under `Settings > Pages` (Deploy from Branch: `main` / `/root`) to host it for free.
